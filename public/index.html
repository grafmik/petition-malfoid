Our request

To: Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive

We, the undersigned players, ask Rockstar Games to cast Malfoid as the female protagonist of Grand Theft Auto VI, in place of the current lead.

Malfoid is the female reimagining of Draco Malfoy that spread across social media in the fall of 2026. In a few weeks she became one of the most drawn and shared characters online, with fan art reaching hundreds of thousands of people and coverage in mainstream entertainment media.

We believe she would make a strong lead for the series:

  • Proven audience. Few characters have gathered such a large and active community so quickly. That audience is already engaged and ready to follow her.
  • A clear personality. Ambitious, proud and morally ambiguous, she fits the tone of a Grand Theft Auto story naturally.
  • A recognizable identity. Her look is instantly identifiable, which matters for a lead character in a game of this scale.
  • Listening to players. Grand Theft Auto has always reflected internet culture. This is an opportunity to show that player voices are heard.

We understand that this request raises questions of licensing and production. We ask Rockstar Games to consider it seriously, to explore an agreement with the rights holders if needed, and to respond publicly to the players who sign.

If you agree, sign below and share this petition.
